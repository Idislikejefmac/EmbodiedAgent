## Second walk (14:53 to 15:00), anchor test

Jeff placed an anchor on a drum stool in the cluttered music room, walked out through the hallway into the great room and kitchen, around, back through the hallway, into the powder room and back to the music room. ARCore had "forgotten" the room and the first anchor was gone. ARCore's log shows two map identities (switch between 14:55:38 and 14:56:21) and 23 of 31 tracking-fault episodes in 14:56 to 14:57, one of about 1 s. So a fast move into the small dark room likely caused a silent map reset. A second anchor on the same spot, placed in the new map, stayed put across a hallway, great room, hallway, music room loop. Distance from the real spot was not measured. Battery 55% to 52%, temperature 33.3 C to 36.5 C.

The anchor-return pass criterion (within about 0.5 m) was therefore not formally measured. G8 measures return-to-start error with our own code.
