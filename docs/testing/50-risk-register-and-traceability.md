### 3.5 Drift check: a card cannot be marked done on plans

`tools/trace.py check` (run by `tests/test_trace.py` and at step 0g) fails when a card in `status.toml` is `done` and any of these holds:

1. A promise P1 to P5 that applies has no Existing automated test and no Manual procedure with a recorded result.
2. A risk scored 15 or more owned by the card has no Existing automated test, and no live `[[waiver]]` (doc 00 section 7).
3. A risk scored 8 to 14 has neither an Existing test nor a recorded Manual procedure.
4. A test listed as Existing does not resolve to a collected test.
5. A security-marked test for the card is skipped in this run.
6. The card's X-01 registration is missing.
7. A lint baseline entry names the card (doc 00 section 4.5).

Applied today to the built cards (from 1.4): P0-06 fails rule 2 on R-FND-57 (20); P0-02 fails rule 2 on R-FND-06 (16); P0-05 fails rule 2 on R-FND-18 (16). P0-01 and P0-03 have no risk of 15 or more. A waiver is the only way to mark P0-02 done before G8 (R-FND-06).

## 6. Maintenance, review cadence, and what this design cannot prove

### 6.1 Cadence

| When | What | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every commit | Quick suite (not `slow`, not `real_vendor`), lints, mypy (required), trace check for touched cards | pre-commit or pre-push hook |
| End of every card | The card's section lists tests that exist; `status.toml` changes only when `trace.py check` passes; risk rows updated with real test IDs; review by a different model | building session plus a review session |
| Every gate | Step 0 in full (2.1); gate record with the contents in 2.2; R-SYS register and `DECISIONS.md` reviewed | Jeff |
| Weekly while active | Full chaos, longer soak, `pip-audit`, backup age | scheduled |
| Quarterly and before the return date | Migration drill (T-SYS-012); review of `docs/privacy.md` | Jeff |
| After a major change | New boundary in doc 00 section 3 in the same change; X-01 registration; red-team run at capped cost | building session |
| On any incident | Failing test first, fix, one risk row added or updated, corpus extended | Jeff |
| After each phase | Re-run the scripts of 1.3, 1.4, 3.6 and 7.3; merge duplicates; re-score; close gaps | Jeff plus one review session |

### 6.2 What the whole test design cannot prove

| Cannot prove | Why | What compensates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| That the agent never acts on injected content | Infinite input space; the model is statistical | Nothing harmful is reachable from the app channel; corpus at every gate (R-SYS-06) |
| That the model never says something false, private or harmful | Free speech is outside the registry | Data-level rules (names withheld below probable, traceable numbers, claims framed as claims); monitors; human review at gates |
| Absolute recognition error rates | Few people and sessions, dependent trials, half the trials go to calibration | Bands, margins, asking on doubt, both modalities plus the challenge for sensitive tools (doc 30 section 2.2) |
| Resistance to a live voice clone answering the challenge | The challenge defeats replay, not live synthesis | Face must agree; actions restorable; the console for anything irreversible |
| That consent was informed and freely given | A human matter | Plain consent script, easy withdrawal, suspension by anyone |
| Deletion of copies outside `data/` | Out of the code's reach | Deletion record lists them; BitLocker; migration checklist |
| What the employer's tools do with files | Outside the project | Jeff-only on this laptop; encrypt from the first byte; move early |
| ARCore correctness in rooms not walked | Vendor behaviour; silent resets look like continuity | Suspect rules, marker, recorded reset fixture |
| Physical safety of a robot from ten runs | 0 of 10 bounds the failure rate only below 25.9 percent | Safety functions below the brain; capped and supervised until the demand counts of DEC-035 |
| Absence of bugs | Fuzzing and mutation find some, not all | Independent reviews; regression-first defect handling |
